User Manual

Flocabulary Master helps you build vocabulary, revise meanings, and practise synonym/antonym questions. Use the navigation menu to switch between sections.

1. Practice Quiz

Questions are drawn from words with frequency 2 or higher and at least one saved synonym or antonym. Frequency-1 words may appear as answer choices. Choose an option to see the explanation, then select Next Question.

2. Word Directory

Search by English word or Bangla meaning. Tap a word to view its details. Use + to increase its frequency and − to decrease it (the minimum is 0). Higher-frequency eligible words are more likely to be selected for a quiz.

3. Add a Word

Enter one English word and choose Fetch & Save Word. The app checks several independent online sources: a dictionary for definitions, Datamuse/WordNet for synonyms and antonyms, Wiktionary as a definition/etymology fallback, and MyMemory for a Bangla translation. Each source can fail independently, so partial results are saved instead of losing everything when one service is unavailable. Review translations and word relationships because online data can be incomplete or sense-dependent. An internet connection is required for lookups.

If lookup fails: check your connection, try the base form of the word (for example, go instead of went), or add the word first and use Edit Word Details to enter missing information manually. Some browsers, file viewers, networks, or ad blockers may block external requests.

4. Frequency system

A new word starts at frequency 1. Adding an existing word again increases its frequency by 1 rather than creating a duplicate. A word becomes quiz-eligible at frequency 2 only if it also has at least one synonym or antonym.

5. Your saved data

Words and theme settings are saved on this device in this browser's local storage. The quiz and already-saved details work offline; adding words or downloading missing details requires internet. Data is not synced online. Clearing browser/site data, using private browsing, or opening the file in a different browser may make saved changes unavailable. Use Download Vocabulary Backup regularly and keep the JSON file somewhere safe.

Backup / Restore your device database

Export a JSON backup to your device before clearing browser data or changing browsers. Restore that file here to recover your saved vocabulary. Your vocabulary is stored locally in this browser; it is not automatically synced between devices.

6. Online data sources and limits

Lookups use Free Dictionary API for definitions, Datamuse (which includes WordNet relationships) for synonyms/antonyms, Wiktionary as a fallback, and MyMemory for optional machine translation. These are third-party services, not a guaranteed or fully verified source; results depend on connectivity, service availability, and whether the word exists in each source. Datamuse usage is acknowledged here as required by its documentation.

8. Theme and navigation

Open Menu to navigate, then use Toggle Theme to switch between light and dark mode. Tap the Flocabulary title to return to the quiz.
